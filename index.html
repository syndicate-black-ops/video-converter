Syndicate Black Ops video converter

Turn a video into an .ogv file that plays in the game, or its sound into an .ogg. Your video never leaves your computer, because the conversion runs in your browser. The Video page explains the format, its limits and how to use the file in a map.

Drop a video here or click to choose a file
  • Width in pixels, up to 2048, and the height follows the shape of the video
  • Frame rate stays the same if you leave it empty, and 30 halves the work for a 60 fps video
  • Bitrate sets the file size: 1100 makes about 8.5 MB per minute, 600 about 4.7 MB
  • Sound quality goes from 0 to 10, and 3 is about 112 kbit/s
